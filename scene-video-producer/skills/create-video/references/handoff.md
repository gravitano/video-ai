# Production and editing handoff

## Brief fields

Topic, audience, platform, duration, objective, key message, CTA, VO language, visual tone, aspect ratio.

## Shot sheet columns

| Shot | Time in–out | Purpose | Visual/keyframe | VO | Overlay | Subject/camera motion | SFX | Asset/take status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use continuous, non-overlapping time ranges. Sum durations. Capture VO wording verbatim in the sheet and retain one approved version.

## Visual continuity

Character face, hair, outfit, room layout, desk/props, palette, time of day, lighting direction, and camera language. Note intentional changes. Inspect keyframes as a contact sheet before generating clips.

## Edit order

1. Record or generate the approved VO and trim breaths as needed.
2. Assemble a rough cut against the timed shot sheet.
3. Insert selected clips and B-roll, trimming to beats; avoid forcing a generated clip to fill its whole nominal duration.
4. Add readable overlays in the editor rather than embedding words in generative imagery.
5. Add sound cues, mix music beneath voice, match color/exposure across clips.
6. Preview on a phone-sized canvas and check captions against app UI safe areas.
7. Export for the chosen platform; for a standard Reels handoff use 1080 × 1920, 9:16, 30 fps, H.264 unless a user/platform spec says otherwise.

## Final report

List approved images, selected video takes, VO, edit project, exported video, and remaining production steps accurately. Do not describe prompts or still images as finished video.
