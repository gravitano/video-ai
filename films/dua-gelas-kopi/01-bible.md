Status: approved

- Rasio: 9:16
- Durasi target: 90
- Batas klip: 8
- Bahasa dialog: Indonesia
- Mode narasi: in-video

## Style
Live-action sinematik, hangat, dan intim. Palet: biru senja yang dingin di luar vs kuning tungsten yang hangat di dalam warung, sebagai metafora "rumah".

<!-- LOCK:STYLE -->
```text
Cinematic live-action film still, shot on 35mm film with anamorphic-style shallow depth of field, naturalistic Indonesian drama, cool blue dusk tones outside contrasted with warm amber tungsten light, soft film grain, gentle halation, photorealistic, emotionally quiet.
```

```text
Ibu Sarmi, a 60-year-old Javanese Indonesian woman, small and slightly stooped, warm wrinkled face, grey-streaked black hair tied in a low bun, thin gold stud earrings, wearing a faded brown sogan batik long-sleeve blouse and a dark brown batik sarong, a thin cream kitchen cloth over her left shoulder.
```

## Props kunci
- **Dua gelas kopi tubruk**: gelas kaca bening bertangkai, tatakan kecil, kopi hitam berasap. Harus selalu terlihat sama.
- **Ransel** Raka: sengaja **tidak** masuk LOCK:CHAR karena posisinya berubah. Ditulis per shot: `a worn brown canvas backpack on his left shoulder` (S01–S12), lalu `his worn brown canvas backpack lies on the floor beside the bench` (S15–S16).

<!-- LOCK:PROP:Kopi -->
```text
two identical clear glass mugs of black Javanese kopi tubruk with small glass saucers, thin steam rising
```

## Aturan tetap
- Tidak ada teks, logo, subtitle, atau tulisan yang terbaca di gambar dan video (termasuk papan nama warung). Semua teks ditambahkan di editor.
- Maksimal 2 karakter per frame; figuran selalu buram di latar belakang.
- Tanpa musik di klip. Musik (gitar akustik atau piano pelan) ditambahkan saat editing.
- Kontinuitas: jalan dan tanah selalu basah; Raka sedikit basah gerimis (rambut lembap); jam dinding tidak perlu terbaca.
